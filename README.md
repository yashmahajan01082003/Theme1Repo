# 🌿 Paryavaran Rakshak

> **Theme:** Climate Education
> **Tagline:** *Let’s Make The World Green Again*
> **Developed By:** Team Dreamers
> **Institution:** Vishwakarma Institute Of Technology, Pune
> **Hackathon:** PCCOE International Grand Challenge 2025

---

# 📖 Overview

**Paryavaran Rakshak** is a gamified climate education platform designed to transform the way students understand nature, sustainability, and environmental responsibility.

Instead of traditional theoretical learning, the platform uses:

* Interactive storytelling
* Psychological engagement
* Gamification
* VR experiences
* Games and quizzes
* Progressive environmental themes

The goal is not just to educate students about climate change — but to emotionally connect them with nature and inspire sustainable action.

---

# 🌍 Problem Statement

Environmental awareness among students remains low because existing educational approaches are:

* Too theoretical
* Not emotionally engaging
* Lacking practical interaction
* Unable to motivate sustainable habits

### Key Challenges

* Students do not understand the real-world impact of climate change.
* Current tools fail to create emotional attachment toward nature.
* Youth participation in environmental practices is limited.
* Sustainable habits are not being formed early.

---

# 🎯 Vision

To create a climate education ecosystem that feels more like a game than a traditional learning application.

The platform focuses on:

* Changing mindset
* Creating emotional connection with nature
* Encouraging eco-friendly behavior
* Building environmental responsibility in future generations

---

# 🧠 Core Philosophy

Instead of focusing only on fancy features, Paryavaran Rakshak focuses on **human psychology**.

The platform aims to create:

| Emotion        | Purpose                                        |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Sympathy       | Help students feel the pain of nature          |
| Fear           | Show consequences of environmental destruction |
| Attachment     | Build connection with plants and Earth         |
| Motivation     | Encourage eco-friendly habits                  |
| Responsibility | Inspire students to take action                |
| Confidence     | Make students believe they can create change   |

# 🧭 Platform Flow

2. Student enters a specific environmental theme.
3. Interactive content and games are unlocked.
4. User earns eco-points after completing activities.
5. New themes unlock progressively.
6. Emotional engagement grows with progress.
7. Students develop long-term eco-friendly habits.

# 🌱 Theme 1 — Polluted Theme

## 🎯 Objective

The **Polluted Theme** is the first and most emotionally impactful stage of the application.

Its purpose is to create:

* Sympathy toward nature
* Emotional discomfort
* Awareness of environmental destruction
* Curiosity to improve the world

This theme acts as the starting point of the student’s climate awareness journey.

---

## 🌫️ Theme Concept

The environment shown in this theme is:

* Dark
* Polluted
* Dry
* Lifeless
* Filled with smoke and waste

Students experience the consequences of:

* Deforestation
* Air pollution
* Plastic waste
* Climate change
* Water contamination

The visual environment is intentionally uncomfortable to emotionally connect students with environmental problems.

---

## 🎮 Theme Features

### 🧩 Interactive Activities

* Pollution cleanup mini-games
* Sorting recyclable and non-recyclable waste
* Identifying polluted areas
* Carbon footprint challenges

### 📚 Educational Content

* Real-world pollution statistics
* Climate disaster case studies
* Videos showing environmental destruction
* Interactive storytelling modules

### 🥽 VR Experiences

Students can:

* Walk through polluted cities
* Experience smog-filled environments
* Witness destroyed forests
* Observe polluted rivers and oceans

---

## 🧠 Psychological Goal

This theme is designed to trigger:

| Emotion        | Effect                                  |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Sympathy       | Makes students emotionally connected    |
| Fear           | Shows future environmental consequences |
| Guilt          | Encourages self-reflection              |
| Responsibility | Motivates students to act               |

The platform uses emotional storytelling rather than textbook learning.

---

## 🏅 Rewards & Progression

Students earn:

* Eco-points
* Theme badges
* XP boosts
* Unlocks for the next environmental stage

Completing the Polluted Theme unlocks the **Plantless Theme**.
